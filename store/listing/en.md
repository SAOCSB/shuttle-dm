---
Shuttle DM is a fast download manager that runs entirely in your browser.

MULTI-CONNECTION DOWNLOADS
• Splits large files into parts and downloads them over several connections at once.
• When a connection finishes early, it takes over half of the largest remaining part, so a download never ends on one slow connection.
• Pause and resume at any time. Downloads also continue after a network drop, a browser crash or a restart.

WEB VIDEOS
• Detects the video and audio a page plays: MP4, WebM and other files, HLS (M3U8) streams, and separate audio and video tracks.
• Hover over a video and click “Download video”, or pick it from the toolbar popup.
• Choose the quality of adaptive streams. HLS streams are saved as standard MP4 files without re-encoding, and separate audio and video tracks are merged into one MP4.
• Records live HLS streams until you stop them.

MORE TOOLS
• Takes over the browser’s own downloads (archives, installers, documents and more) to speed them up. You choose the file types and minimum size, and can exclude sites.
• Saves complete pages as a single MHTML file, or as HTML.
• Batch download: lists every link, image, audio and video on a page or in your selection, with type filters and wildcard search.
• A download manager with categories, search, sorting, keyboard shortcuts and a live view of every connection.
• Global speed limit, download queue, folders by file type, notifications and dark mode.
• Available in English, 简体中文, Français, 日本語 and 한국어.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your computer. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties. The source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/SAOCSB/shuttle-dm

PLEASE NOTE
• Only download content you have the right to save, and respect the terms of the websites you use.
• Videos protected by DRM (such as Widevine, FairPlay or PlayReady) can’t be downloaded.
• Videos on YouTube are not supported.
• Browsers allow up to 6 connections to one HTTP/1.1 server, which is why 6 connections per download is the default.
